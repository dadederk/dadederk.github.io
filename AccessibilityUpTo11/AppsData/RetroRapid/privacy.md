# Privacy Policy for RetroRapid!

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

RetroRapid! is designed to be played without creating an account with the developer. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

## Data Collection

RetroRapid! does **not** use developer-operated analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The developer does not collect, sell, or use personal information from the app for advertising, and the app does not send gameplay data to a developer-operated server.

## Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores information locally so that it can work as expected, including:

- gameplay, sound, haptic, control, accessibility, and visual-style preferences;
- the daily free-run allowance and the date on which it resets;
- local personal bests and progress needed for Game Center scores and achievements; and
- a cached indication that Unlimited Plays has been purchased, used while StoreKit checks the current entitlement.

Deleting the app removes this local information. Information managed by Apple, such as a purchase, Game Center score, or achievement, may remain associated with your Apple Account.

## Game Center

When Game Center is enabled, RetroRapid! uses Apple's service to authenticate the local player, submit scores and achievement progress, and read leaderboard information such as player display names, avatars, and best scores. The app uses this information to present leaderboards and friend markers; it does not copy it to a developer-operated server or create a separate player account.

Apple handles Game Center identity and service data according to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and your Game Center settings. You can manage Game Center in your device's Settings.

## SharePlay

When you choose a SharePlay friend race, RetroRapid! sends the information needed to run the match to the other participants through Apple's Group Activities service. This can include participant display names, readiness, selected difficulty, match progress, scores, remaining lives, and results. RetroRapid! does not send this information to a developer-operated server and does not receive the audio or video from an associated FaceTime call.

Apple manages SharePlay, FaceTime, Messages, and Group Activities data according to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and your device settings.

## Purchases

Unlimited Plays is processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment details. The app checks Apple's signed StoreKit transactions on your device to determine whether Unlimited Plays and Family Sharing access are available. Purchases and Family Sharing are managed by Apple and are subject to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Diagnostics

RetroRapid! uses Apple's unified logging system for privacy-safe technical diagnostics on your device. The app does not include a third-party crash reporter. Apple may make crash or diagnostic information available to the developer when your device and App Store settings allow it; Apple controls that collection and sharing.

## Contacting the Developer

If you email the developer for support, the developer receives your email address and the information you include in the message. It is used only to respond, provide support, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations, and is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal information contained in support correspondence, including access, correction, or deletion. Contact the developer to make a request. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority; in the United Kingdom, this is the Information Commissioner's Office.

## Children's Privacy

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. Game Center, SharePlay, and App Store purchases are Apple services and may be subject to parental controls and family settings managed by Apple.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app changes in a way that affects how information is handled. The latest version will remain available on this page.
