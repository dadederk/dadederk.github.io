# Privacy Policy for Xarra

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

This policy explains how Xarra handles information in the app and how we handle information you deliberately send when contacting us.

## The Short Version
Xarra does not require an account and its developer does not receive your documents, listening history, or app-usage data. The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking. Your documents are stored on your device and can sync through your private iCloud account when you enable iCloud.

Some features make user-directed connections to Apple or to a source you choose, such as a website you import or Project Gutenberg. Those connections are described below.

## Information the Developer Does Not Collect from the App
Xarra does not send the following information to its developer:

- the text or files you import;
- your library, listening position, bookmarks, tags, or categories;
- analytics, advertising identifiers, or a profile of how you use the app; or
- payment-card or Apple Account details.

There are no Xarra accounts, ads, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

## Local Storage and iCloud Sync
Audio Documents, library metadata, preferences, and listening progress are stored on your device. If you enable iCloud, eligible documents and progress can synchronise through Apple's private CloudKit service using your iCloud account. We do not have access to that private database.

You can delete documents in Xarra. You can manage the app's iCloud data through your device's iCloud settings. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by [Apple's iCloud Terms and Conditions](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/icloud/) and [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Content You Choose to Import
Files and text you add from your device are processed locally. When you enter or share a URL, Xarra connects directly to that website to retrieve the content you requested. The website operator may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and request details under its own privacy policy.

When you browse or import from the Gutenberg Library, Xarra connects to catalogue and content services operated by Gutendex or Project Gutenberg. Those services may receive the ordinary information needed to fulfil the request. Xarra does not send them your existing library or listening history.

## Apple Intelligence
On supported devices, Xarra's current Apple Intelligence features use Apple's Foundation Models on device for tasks such as summarisation, suggested tags and categories, and audio-friendly explanations of code. We do not receive the source content, prompts, or results. Imports and listening continue to work when these models are unavailable.

## Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, and System Features
On supported systems, Xarra can make limited library metadata available to Apple's system features so you can find or act on a document. This can include a title, safe source label and type, summary, tags, categories, dates, labelled playback progress, and a small local artwork image.

Xarra excludes document body text, selected text, bookmarks, raw source URLs, and logs from this system-facing representation. Voice requests and other interactions with Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, iCloud, Now Playing, and related system services are also subject to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Purchases and Subscriptions
Apple processes purchases, subscriptions, Family Sharing, offer-code redemption, and refunds through the App Store. StoreKit verifies access on your device. We do not receive your payment information or a personally identifiable purchase record from the app.

## Diagnostics and Support
Xarra's runtime logs are designed to avoid document content and other private values. Logs and diagnostics are not automatically sent to us. If you choose to contact support and share a message, screenshot, log, or other attachment, we receive the information you deliberately provide and use it to respond to your request.

## Open-Source Software
Xarra includes open-source libraries for tasks such as parsing web content, EPUB and DAISY documents, and compressed files. These libraries run as part of the app and do not independently provide your content to us. Their licences and acknowledgements are available in Xarra.

## Support and Institutional-Access Enquiries
If you email us, we receive your email address and the information in your message. For an institutional-access enquiry, we may ask for the organisation's name, country and type, an institutional email address, a public website or registry entry, the approximate number of people who need access, and a short description of the intended use.

We use this information only to respond, provide support, assess eligibility, prevent misuse, and administer access. We do not sell it or use it for unrelated marketing. Information normally comes directly from you; for an institutional-access enquiry, we may also check public organisational information that you provide or direct us to. Email providers process messages as needed to deliver and store the correspondence. We otherwise share enquiry information only when necessary to provide the requested access, comply with the law, or protect legal rights.

For support and general enquiries, our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding and resolving the request. When you ask us to provide institutional access, processing is necessary to take steps at your request and to administer any resulting arrangement. We rely on our legitimate interests to prevent misuse and keep proportionate programme records, and on a legal obligation where the law requires a record or disclosure.

We retain correspondence and programme records only while they are reasonably needed to resolve the enquiry, operate issued access, prevent duplicate or improper distribution, or meet legal obligations, and then delete or anonymise them.

## Children's Privacy
Xarra does not require an account and does not knowingly collect children's personal data through the app. If a child, parent, or guardian contacts us directly, we handle the information in that message as described under Diagnostics and Support.

## Your Rights
Depending on where you live and the lawful basis involved, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of personal information you have sent us. These rights are not absolute in every situation. Contact us using the address below to make a request. If you are in the United Kingdom, you may also complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

## Changes to This Policy
We will update this policy when Xarra or our information-handling practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Where required, we will provide additional notice before a material change takes effect.
