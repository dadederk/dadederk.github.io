RetroRapid! is a fast three-lane arcade racer where players dodge traffic,
chase high scores, and race friends live with SharePlay. It is available on
iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch.

The game is free to download. It includes nine solo plays on the first day,
then three per day. Unlimited Plays is a US $2.99 one-time purchase, with
regional pricing and no subscription. SharePlay friend races and Apple Watch
play are always free.

The nine screenshots are full-resolution images for editorial coverage of
RetroRapid. The files are named by platform and subject. Two approved App Store
in-app event illustrations are also included. The Classic app icon is provided
as 1024, 512, and 256 pixel PNGs.

RetroRapid does not collect data and has no ads, analytics, or tracking. Solo
racing works offline. Ten percent of proceeds are donated to AMMEC, a
Valencia-based nonprofit supporting people with physical disabilities and
their families.
